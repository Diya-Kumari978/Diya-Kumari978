## 🛡️ Featured Project — PocketShield

<div align="center">
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Hackathon%20Project-FF61D2?style=for-the-badge" />
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/AI%20Powered-00C9A7?style=for-the-badge" />

> A hackathon safety app that detects emergency situations through **phone shake patterns and hand gestures** — no camera network or internet required for detection. AI confirms real emergencies to avoid false alarms, then silently sends the user's **live location and a short video clip** to a trusted contact via WhatsApp, with no visible sign on screen.
